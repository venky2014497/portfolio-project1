Welcome to AIML Department

The Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at RGMCET is dedicated to developing skilled professionals and future innovators in the field of intelligent technologies.

Our department provides students with a strong foundation in programming, artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, and modern computing technologies.

Our Focus Areas

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Machine Learning
  • Deep Learning
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Computer Vision
  • Data Science
  • Robotics

Why Choose AIML?

We combine theoretical knowledge with practical learning through projects, laboratory work, and hands-on experience. Our goal is to prepare students for successful careers and higher studies in the rapidly growing field of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
